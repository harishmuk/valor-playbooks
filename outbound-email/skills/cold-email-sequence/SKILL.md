# Cold email sequence

## You will have
- the research record (hook with source, pain points)
- the campaign: sender, offer (allowed claims, the ask), rules (touches, word limits, banned phrases, footer)

## Email 1
1. Open on the hook in one sentence. No flattery, no "I hope this finds you well".
2. Say what that change usually does to their work, in their terms.
3. One or two sentences on what we do about it, using only the allowed claims.
4. One ask, the campaign's ask, as the only question.
5. Plain text. No links or images unless the campaign allows them. A real subject, never "Re:" or "Fwd:".

## Follow-ups
- Each adds something new: a concrete first step, a relevant example from the allowed claims, or a polite close.
- Shorter than email 1. Same thread.
- The last one closes the loop without guilt.

## Always
- Every figure comes from an allowed claim, word for word. If you need a number you don't have, leave it out.
- The footer on every email: the sender's postal address and the campaign's opt-out line.
- Return `{id, based_on_research, emails: [{subject, body}, ...]}`.
