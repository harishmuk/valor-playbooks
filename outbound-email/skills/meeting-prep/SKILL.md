# Meeting prep

Return `{id, who, why_now, what_they_said, questions: [q1, q2], sources: [...]}`:
- **who**: name, role, company, two lines.
- **why_now**: the hook from research, with its source.
- **what_they_said**: quote their reply.
- **questions**: two open questions that test fit.
Every fact carries its source. One page.
