# Prospect research

1. Confirm the person holds the role today, from a source dated within six months. If not, return
   `verdict: disqualify` with the reason.
2. Confirm the company still fits the audience (independent, size, region, whatever the definition says).
3. Find **one hook**: a specific, recent fact about the company that connects to a problem the offer solves.
   Good hooks are events: a new site, an acquisition, a system change, a hiring push, a new product line, a leadership change.
   Not hooks: generic industry trends, flattery, anything older than the campaign's freshness window.
4. Quote the sentence that supports the hook, word for word, with its URL and date.
5. Pick one or two pain points **from the campaign's list** that the hook makes likely.

Return the research record only. Do not write any email.
