# Reply writing

- Answer what they said first, in one or two sentences.
- If they are interested, offer the campaign's ask with two or three concrete times or a booking link if the campaign allows links.
- Only allowed claims. If they ask something the claims don't cover, say the sender will follow up; do not guess.
- Plain text, under the campaign's reply word limit, with the footer.
- Return `{id, subject, body}`.
